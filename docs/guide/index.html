How Soodo works

Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and other assistants recommend products they can read and trust. Soodo checks each of your products the way those systems see them, then gives you a short list of fixes, most important first.

Scanning and checking never change your store. The only thing Soodo writes is a product description, and only after you approve the exact wording. Every previous version is saved and can be restored.

HighFix first. Blocks discovery.
MediumWeakens how products are matched.
LowPolish once the rest is done.

The workflow

  1. 1
    Scan catalogImports products, variants, categories and store policies. Read-only.
  2. 2
    Check live pagesOpens each published product page and robots.txt, the way a crawler does.
  3. 3
    Fix issuesWork through the fix plan. Most fixes are made in Shopify; missing specifications are added through Soodo.
  4. 4
    Review and publishApprove the proposed description. Soodo publishes it and checks the result.
01Home screen

Overview

The Overview answers two questions: how ready is my catalog, and what should I do next? Start here every time.

  1. 1
    Catalog readinessThe average score of your active products, and how much it changed in the last 30 days. 100 means every check that could run has passed.
  2. 2
    Next stepThe single most useful thing to do now. Press the button; Soodo takes you there or starts the job.
  3. 3
    Setup progressImport catalog, check live pages, fix issues. A tick means the step is done.
  4. 4
    Readiness by areaShare of products that pass each area. “Run live checks to measure” means that area waits on step 2. Click a tile to see its fixes.
  5. 5
    Scan catalogRun a new scan after you change products in Shopify.
  1. 1
    Readiness over timeOne reading per day, saved after scans, live checks and published changes.
  2. 2
    Products by readinessHow many products are Strong, Good, Fair or Weak, so a good average can’t hide weak products.
  3. 3
    Top fixesThe five fixes with the most impact. “2/3” means two of your three active products are affected.
  4. 4
    Needs attentionProducts with the lowest score. Click one to open it.
  5. 5
    Store-wide signalsPolicies, contact details and crawler access that apply to every product.
  6. 6
    Recent changesThe latest descriptions you published or restored.
  1. 1
    Daily detailThe date, the readiness score, how many products were fully ready and how many fixes were open that day.

  2. 2

05Step 3 · in Soodo

Adding specifications

Assistants answer buyers’ questions from what is written on the page. Soodo finds the key facts for each type of product and helps you write the missing ones into the description.

  1. 1
    Where you areAdd verified facts, review the draft, publish.
  2. 2
    FoundAlready known from your product data. The source is shown underneath. These fields are locked; change them in Shopify.
  3. 3
    MissingType the value only if you have verified it. Leave anything you don’t know empty.
  4. 4
    ConfirmTick to confirm the values are accurate.
  5. 5
    Create description draftMakes a draft. Nothing changes in your store yet.
  1. 1
    Buyer questionsWhat an assistant could answer from your facts. “Stored fact only” means the fact exists in Shopify but may not show on the page, so add it to the description. Soodo never guesses an answer.
06Step 4

Review and publish

You always see the exact wording before anything is published. Soodo keeps your existing text and adds one clear specifications list at the end.

  1. 1
    CurrentThe description in your store today.
  2. 2
    ProposedYour text, plus the specifications list.
  3. 3
    SourcesWhere each value came from: your product data, or you.
  4. 4
    ApproveRequired before publishing.
  5. 5
    PublishSoodo re-reads the product, publishes, then reads it back to confirm the change landed. In a real store the button says Publish to Shopify.
  6. 6
    Reject draftDiscards the draft. Your product is not changed.
  1. 1
    Published and verifiedThe change is live in Shopify and has been checked.
  2. 2
    All facts foundThe specifications now come from the description itself, and the score goes up.
If the product changedIf someone edits the product in Shopify after the draft was made, Soodo stops and asks you to create a fresh draft. It never overwrites newer edits.

?When something looks wrong

Troubleshooting

Publishing shows an error

Before publishing, Soodo re-reads the product from Shopify to make sure nothing changed. If Shopify refuses that read, the publish stops and nothing is changed.

The most common cause is a permission that hasn’t been approved yet. Open Soodo from your Shopify admin and accept the permission request, then try again. Settings shows whether each permission is approved.

If it still fails, send a screenshot of the error message itself.

“Shopify did not return a public Online Store URL”

The product isn’t available on the Online Store sales channel, so it has no public page. Search engines and assistants can’t see it, and Soodo can’t live-check it. If the product should be for sale, open it in Shopify and make it available on the Online Store channel.

Some checks say “Not checked yet”

They depend on a step that hasn’t run. Visibility checks need a live check; category, barcode and price checks need a scan from the current version. Run Scan catalog, then Check live pages.

The scan stopped part way (“Partly imported”)

Press Continue scan; finished batches are kept. If it stops with an error, read the message. A permission message means you need to approve the updated permissions first.

An area tile shows “—”

No product has had all of that area’s checks run yet. Soodo won’t claim a pass it hasn’t measured. The note under the tile says which step to run.

History says “Stopped: product changed”

The description in Shopify changed while Soodo was publishing. Your current content was kept. Review the product and create a fresh draft.

Does a score of 100 guarantee I’ll appear in AI answers?

No. Readiness measures what Soodo can verify: that pages are reachable, data is complete and consistent, and facts are written clearly. Those make products easier to find and recommend, but no tool can guarantee a ranking.

A–ZTerms used in the app

Glossary

Readiness
A score from 0 to 100. High-priority checks count three times, medium twice, low once. Checks that couldn’t run are left out.
Active product
A product with status Active in Shopify. Draft and archived products are left out of the fix plan.
Shopify product category
The standard category from Shopify’s product taxonomy, such as Apparel › Coats. Different from the free-text product type.
Category attributes
Fields like color, fabric or size that belong to a category. Filled in on the product in Shopify.
GTIN / barcode
The manufacturer’s barcode number (UPC, EAN). Lets search engines match the exact item.
Structured data
Hidden product markup in the page that machines read for price, stock and identifiers. Your theme usually adds it.
robots.txt
A file on your store that tells crawlers which pages they may read. Edited in your theme as robots.txt.liquid.
Crawler
A program that reads web pages for a search engine or assistant, such as Googlebot or OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search.
noindex
A tag asking search engines not to list the page.
Canonical link
Tells search engines which address is the main one for a page.
Metafield
An extra field stored on a product in Shopify. Your theme may or may not show it.
Draft
A proposed description waiting for your approval. Nothing changes until you publish it.
